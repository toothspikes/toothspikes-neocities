Falling Forwards (2026)

Yeah yeah it's titktok music idc... I got addicted to ridgeclub. I just love music that makes me FEEL, and ridgeclub gives me an ache in my chest that makes me stop breathing. Music likes this reinforces the close-minded idea in my brain that no one else feels their music the way I feel mine. That's just the chemicals doing their work, but regardless I am in love with ridgeclub's music right now. Since there are no albums, I'll just use my favorite single to fill in the blanks here. I love most of the handful of songs that are out on Spotify though, save for one or two.

Epic Garden Music (1981)

I've been listening to my goth playlist again recently (not actually MY playlist but i luv it, Goth Playlist for Baby Bats from Alienpuke!) and stumbled on this gem. Super catchy, and makes me want to MOVE!! Very nice SAX!!! I'll definitely be checking out the band's other music later, starting with this album very soon!
